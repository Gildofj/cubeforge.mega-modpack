# Installation Guide: cubeforge.mega-modpack

This guide will walk you through installing and configuring **cubeforge.mega-modpack** for your Cube World Steam installation.

---

## 1. Prerequisites

Before installing, ensure you have:
1. **Cube World (Steam Version)** installed and updated.
2. **cubeforge.loader** (recommended) or **Cube World Mod Launcher** by ChrisMiuchiz.
3. **Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64)** installed.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Always back up your save files!**
> Modded content can alter character data or inventory formats.
> Backup path: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CubeWorld\Save\` or `<CubeWorldFolder>\Save\`.

---

## 2. Installation Options

### Option A: Complete Bundle (`cubeforge-megamod.dll`)
1. Download `cubeforge-megamod.dll` from the Releases page.
2. Place it into `<CubeWorldFolder>/Mods/`.
3. Launch the game.

### Option B: Standalone Individual Mods
Choose only the specific features you want:
- `cubeforge-sea-exploration.dll`
- `cubeforge-lore-interactions.dll`
- `cubeforge-combat-updates.dll`
- `cubeforge-creature-updates.dll`
- `cubeforge-shop-updates.dll`
- `cubeforge-world-gen.dll`
- `cubeforge-beginner-mode.dll`
- `cubeforge-region-lock.dll`
- `cubeforge-weapon-upgrades.dll`
- `cubeforge-quest-system.dll`
- `cubeforge-player-updates.dll`
- `cubeforge-stack-updates.dll`

Place your selected `.dll` files into `<CubeWorldFolder>/Mods/`.

---

## 3. Configuration & Verifying Installation

- Open the in-game chat by pressing `Enter`.
- Type `/cubeforge status` to see active modules.
- For all available commands, refer to the [Commands Reference](file:///d:/Projects/CubeMegaMod/docs/COMMANDS.md).
